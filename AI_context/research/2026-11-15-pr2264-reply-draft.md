Thank you for the careful review. Each point is a commit of its own on top of the series, in the order of your list, then the notes.

1. **Notification lost** (`c42de0b26f`). mdnsd sets `ln_Type = NT_MESSAGE` under the `Forbid()` where it keeps the message; the library no longer touches it after the READ.

2. **Own superseded records** (`57a70ae9fe`). In `mdnsCacheRecord()`, a local record with the cache-flush bit ends the other local entries of the same link, name and type (`ce_TTL = 0`, `ce_Expires = now + TICKS`). Two guards as for remote sets: entries refreshed in the last second stay (one packet carries the whole set), and a goodbye does not flush.

3. **`dnssd_reg_rename()` overflow** (`dd460abf9d`). 32-bit counter; a suffix of more than 9 digits is part of the name. The same commit takes your note on `mdns_reg.c:840`: the instance is cut to fit the service's whole name in 255 bytes as well as 63, and when not even " (n)" fits, the registration ends with a conflict, which its client is told. `dnssd_reg_test` covers 999999999, the 20-digit name, and a 255-byte service name.

4. **`DNSSD_CloseSession()`** (`c0d999739d`). The CLOSE message and its reply port are made in `DNSSD_OpenSessionA()`; closing allocates nothing. Called from another task than the opener it does nothing (documented). One difference from your suggestion: the session is freed on `DNSSD_OK`, and also on `DNSSD_ERR_NOENGINE` and `DNSSD_ERR_STALE`. In those two cases no mdnsd holds the notification any more. As it ends, mdnsd replies every lent notification while its port is still public (`dnssdServerCancel()`), acts on no message after that, and answers those still queued with NOENGINE after `RemPort()`. The client's `FindPort()`/`PutMsg()` is under `Forbid()`, so a CLOSE is either drained or never sent. With the task check in place, STALE comes only from another incarnation, i.e. after the old mdnsd ended. Keeping the memory there would leak a session every time a program closes after mdnsd was stopped.

5. **Limits from memory** (`10a9643e15`). mdnsd records `AvailMem(MEMF_ANY)` at start-up (kept as `IPTR`, so a 64-bit host with more than 4 GB is not truncated). Each limit is a share with a floor and a cap: cache 1/128 (16 KB to 256 KB), operations 1/128 (16 to 128; a session half of that), and a new per-session byte budget for queued events, 1/512 (8 KB to 64 KB). Going over the budget is handled like a full queue (dropped, LOST). The first event of an empty queue always queues, so a session holds at most max(budget, one event), and the 16 sessions 1 MB at most.

6. **Stack frame** (`fecdb9658f`). `mine` is static, commented: only mdnsd's task calls it (from `handle_packet()`), and nothing of it outlives the call.

7. **netmidid 0.0.0.0** (`a7b62e6915`). On `present && !seen->address`, `applemidi_discovered()` prints that the peer is out of this transport's reach, leaves `wanted_present` at 0 and returns, as `transport_midi2.c` does.

8. **netmidid read errors** (`2278e68a71`). On a result other than OK, EMPTY, STALE or NOENGINE, `reading` stays 1 and the function returns 0; the next periodic turn reads again.
